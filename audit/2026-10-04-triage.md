# Triage of the 2026-10-04 whole-repo audit

The findings are in `audit/2026-10-04-skills-audit.md` and `audit/2026-10-04-skills-audit-findings.json`. Each fix's
regression test failed on the old code, or killed a mutant that removes the fix, and passes now. A fix in one vault
went into its twins under the same id.

Statuses:
- **FIXED**: code changed, with its commit and test. **FIXED in part** names what is still open.
- **DOCUMENTED**: docs or NatSpec corrected.
- **PENDING**: open. "Owner decision" names the choice the fix needs; the rest have a proposed fix in the JSON.

The two roll-ups:
- **SYS-01** (anyone could starve a verifier to death): FIXED by B5-01 and A5-01, with A4-01 documented. What
  remains is LG-4's own rule: a day in which nobody proves still stops P1 or P2. A4-02 (successor continuity) is
  pending.
- **DEP-01** (non-EVM vault immutability unchecked): in part. B3-02 is fixed (Stellar binds its chain ids to the
  network) and B4-01 is a mandatory, documented pre-listing check; B1-02 (Solana upgrade authority), B2-01 (NEAR's
  initial state) and the verifiers' ELF pin of B1-03 wait on the owner's decision.
