# Red team of 2026-10-31

A red team of `spec/rebalance-state-machine` at `e9c3ad0` (the merge of #31, "IndexZap: ether in and out, and
EIP-2612 permits"), run on 2026-10-31 after the review of the same day (`audit/2026-10-31-review.md`, AU-1 to AU-3).
Its scope was the contracts that hold or move funds (`src/`, `keeper/src/KeeperExecutor.sol`), read whole and
adversarially, with the zap's new ether and permit paths first; the keeper bot and the restock service
(`keeper/keeper.mjs`, `keeper/bot/`, `keeper/restock.mjs`, `keeper/restock/`), read by a delegated reviewer whose
findings were then confirmed at their lines; and the mainnet constants the deploy pins (`script/MainnetAddresses.sol`).

Method: every entry point was traced for reentrancy through token callbacks and the refund and payout of ether, for
what a hostile `tokenIn`, intermediate token or recipient can do to another user's funds, for rounding that could be
turned against the holders, for the auction's state machine under a hostile guardian, executor or flash issuer, and for
the engine's measurement under router strays; then probes were written as tests (`test/RedTeam.t.sol`, kept untracked,
ids `RY-1` to `RY-5`) and the fuzz and invariant suites were run far above their configured depth. The hub's, the
builder's, `MintRequests`'s and the executor's invariants held at 256 runs of 96 calls with no revert
(`fail_on_revert = true`), the leftover `test/Rogue.t.sol` fuzzer at 20,000 runs, and the mainnet constants match the
canonical deployments (Pyth `0x4305…`, Universal Router 2.0.0 `0x66a9…`, PoolManager `0x0000…4444c5…`, WETH, USDC,
USDT, SwapRouter02, Wormhole's Token Bridge).

Each id is `RY-…`: the report was drafted with `RT-1` to `RT-9`, which the red team of 2026-09-24 already holds as
`RT-01` to `RT-25` (`test_RT21_...` among its tests), and was renumbered on 2026-11-01. The probes below keep their
draft names; they moved, untracked, to `audit/poc/2026-10-31-RedTeam.t.sol` and `audit/poc/2026-10-31-Rogue.t.sol`,
out of the build, whose `--deny warnings` the probes' `selfdestruct` failed. The Status column is the review's; the
last section gives each id's status after the fixes of 2026-11-01. A fixed finding's test carries its id. RY-6's unit tests were run against the fix reverted and
failed on `hermes.trusted is not a function`; the restock's wiring is proved by the opt-in end-to-end test
(`keeper/test/e2e/restock.mjs`, archive RPC needed), which this review updated but did not run.

## Checked and clean

- **Hub.** Issue, redeem, `redeemSkipping`, `claim` and the settlement keep the backing invariant with claims under
  every rounding direction; the streaming fee's accrual before each of them keeps it too (the scale rounds down, the
  slack stays in the index). The settlement's callback cannot reach any function that mints, burns, pays a claim or
  touches a rebalance, and `_checkBacking` demands the whole of `amountsIn` on top of what the index held, so donations,
  rounding surplus and `redeemSkipping` claims never fund an executor. `startRebalance`, `cancelRebalance`, the expiry
  and `_permissionlessAt` were walked for every ordering of owner, guardian and stranger. The clone's `ReentrancyGuard`
  (OpenZeppelin 5.7.0, `_status == ENTERED` is the only check) and `Initializable` work from zero storage; the
  implementation refuses `initialize` from anyone but the factory, which is `msg.sender` of its constructor.
- **Factory.** CREATE2 salts carry the creator, so no address can be taken or front-run; terms are copied at creation,
  so a relisting never repoints an index; the creation fee goes to a recipient the owner names and the policy refuses
  the factory and every index as recipient; `Ownable2Step` with `renounceOwnership` disabled; the guardian's removal
  cancellable only by itself.
- **Zap, ether and permits.** The refund and the payout hand control to the caller or `to`, but every entry point is
  guarded, the nonce is checked again before `issue`, and `previewIssue` and `issue` agree within one transaction
  whatever a callback does to the supply or the accrual clock (both read the same post-accrual scale). `_tryPermit`
  swallows a front-run permit and lets the transfer enforce the approval. The route checks make the far end the
  user's token and the pool manager's own settlement refuses any other, so no middle token can be left in the zap or
  taken from it; a malicious intermediate token can only lie about its own balance, which only moves its own units.
- **Builder.** One balance per token covers every escrow: deposits, buys, redeems and sweeps are credited from measured
  deltas of the real token, the router is pre-funded with exactly a leg's amount and holds no allowance, and a seller's
  multi-hop sale of an intermediate another user has escrowed moves only what the seller's previous hop produced.
- **MintRequests.** A request closes before any token moves; the requester's receipt is measured, so a self-fill or a
  fee cannot pass the limit; stables are USDC or USDT only, and neither can be an index.
- **Keeper executor.** `onXindexRebalance` answers only the index named in its own transient `_filling`; every
  allowance the owner sets bounds what the hot keys can move (RS-1, RS-2 held in the invariant suite at 256 × 96);
  every approval the executor grants is consumed in the same call.
- **Keeper bot, otherwise.** `usdcFloor`'s sign, rounding and decimals; `deadline`, `maxSupply` and `maxAmountsIn`;
  one transaction in flight with bounded fee bumps and no double fill across a reorg; keys read from the environment
  only and never logged; the restock's VAA handling (fetched by emitter and sequence from the bot's own receipt,
  re-parsed, verified by the receiving core before storage), `bridgeOut` only to the owner's pinned recipient, the
  journal's crash recovery; request griefing bounded to one reverted fill's gas by the three-strike backoff; no
  prototype pollution, path or command injection, or unbounded memory.
