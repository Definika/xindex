# Logic review of 2026-09-18

A review of the whole repository for logic errors, at `main` `e5e37d9`, after the 2026-09-16 whole-repo audit
(`audit/triage.md`). It read the four core contracts end to end, mutation-tested the offline suite (about 125 hand-picked
mutants of `src/`), instrumented the invariant handler, ran the scripts against a Sepolia fork and the live Sepolia
deployment, and checked the docs against the code. Findings are `LR-01` to `LR-09`; their regression tests carry the id.

Each fix was checked the way `audit/triage.md` asks: the test fails on the old code (or on the mutant it is meant to
catch) and passes on the new.

| ID | Sev | Finding | Disposition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LR-01 | MEDIUM (tests) | Checks after an expected constructor revert never ran | FIXED | Forge 1.8.1 defaults to `dynamic_test_linking = true`, which deploys a `src/` contract a test creates through a cheatcode; a test function then ended, reported as a pass, at the first `vm.expectRevert` followed by `new X(...)`. Five of the six checks in `test_constructor_rejectsABadMarket` (the `OLD` case cited for XA-37 among them) and the zero-router check in the zap's `test_constructor` were dead, and a rule constructor that skipped `_checkPool(usdc, usdPool)` passed the suite. `foundry.toml` now sets `dynamic_test_linking = false`; all tests pass with it off. An unmet `expectCall` is skipped in the broken mode too, so nothing placed after such a revert can detect it: `test_LR01_checksAfterAnExpectedConstructorRevertStillRun` checks the setting first and fails when it is flipped. |
| LR-02 | LOW | Clipped value was handed back to components pinned at the opposite bound | FIXED | `IndexRule._shift` treated every component below its ceiling as able to take a raise, including one just clipped up to its floor for being overweight (mirrored for a lowering pass). With equal prices, weights 10/50/40 and holdings 50/10/40 it returned 28/20/52 where the documented algorithm gives 25/20/55: value-neutral and within bounds, but it sold less of the most overweight component than the bounds allow, the normal case for the first rebalances of an index created with arbitrary units. `_targets` is now a water-filling (`_fill`, `_share`, `_clip`, `_pin`): each pass shares the unpinned value by weight, clips, and pins only the side the clips prove (the floors when they add at least as much value as the ceilings remove, the ceilings otherwise), at most one pass per component. Tests: `test_LR02_aComponentClippedToItsFloorTakesNoValueBack`, `test_LR02_aComponentClippedToItsCeilingIsSharedOutAgain`, `testFuzz_LR02_targetsAreAWaterFilling` (all three fail on the old rule; the fuzz also passes 20,000 runs). The old fuzz checked bounds and value, never shape. The rule shrank by 193 bytes and the factory's initcode headroom grew to 2,657 bytes. Fork rebalances pass at block 26,005,292. This changes the recorded methodology's maths, at the owner's request of 2026-09-18; docs: `IndexRule` header, `IIndexRule.targetUnits`, ARCHITECTURE §2 and §4, AGENTS.md product decisions. |
| LR-03 | LOW (ops) | The Sepolia deployment predates the audit fixes and the repo's tooling cannot drive it | DOCUMENTED | The factory at `0x3541…2CB1` is from commit `8c28c7d`: its `pendingTokenConfig` returns a three-field struct and `pendingIsListing` reverts (read on-chain 2026-09-18), so `LISTING_MODE=execute` and `status` fail decoding, and `abi/*.json` do not describe it. It also lacks the XA-01 snapshot checks. README now says so, gives the direct `executeListing` call that still works, and names the remedy, a redeploy from current code. The listing window of the old deployment closes 2026-09-25 13:05 UTC. The redeploy needs the deployer keystore and the Safe, so it is the owner's to do. |
| LR-04 | LOW (fixture) | The fork executor's `fill` could be pointed at a fake index | FIXED | `RebalanceExecutorV3.fill` was open to anyone and trusted any address, so a contract posing as an index called back from its own `executeRebalance` and took the whole WETH float; the XA-12 test only tried a direct callback. `fill` is now deployer-only and settles only an index the factory registered (`NotIndex`); the constructor takes the factory. Test: `test_LR04_rebalanceExecutorV3_fillCannotBePointedAtAFakeIndex`. README notes both limits. |
| LR-05 | LOW (tests) | Seven mutants of `src/` and the scripts passed the whole suite | FIXED | New tests, each checked against its mutant: a restart after an unfilled auction that kept the expired targets (`test_LR05_aRestartAfterAnUnfilledAuctionTakesAFreshSnapshot`, prices now move between the two snapshots); the XA-22 nonce recheck moved in front of the swaps (`test_LR05_aRebalanceSettledDuringTheRefundStopsTheMint` settles a real rebalance from a callback token's hook during the refund; the old test only mocked two nonce reads); a salt without the token list, the name or the symbol (`test_LR05_addressCommitsToTheTokenListTheNameAndTheSymbol`); `delist` without the moved token's position update, which made delisting that token panic (`test_LR05_aTokenMovedBySwapAndPopCanStillBeDelisted`); a spread that skipped the oldest 10-minute part (new case in `test_targetUnits_snapshotNeedsAgreeingSubWindows`); a leg minimum applied to every hop (`test_LR05_aLegMinimumAppliesToTheLastHopOnly`); and `status`, which only logged, so its test could assert nothing: it now returns the `Report[]` its log lines are written from, and `test_XA42_statusReportsListingsAndPendingProposals` asserts on it. Smaller gaps closed: exactly `MAX_EXCLUDED` exclusions, the last moment of the guardian-change window (`test_LR05_aGuardianChangeExecutesAtTheLastMomentOfItsWindow`), a prank spent on a view, and a comment on an assertion that cannot fail. |
| LR-06 | LOW (tests) | The invariant handler did not model the hold-off after an unfilled auction, and its campaign was shallow | FIXED | A stranger's `startRebalance` after an expiry reverted with `Unauthorized`, swallowed by `fail_on_revert = false`. The handler now waits out the expiry hold-off and requires `canStartRebalance()` to agree; `redeem`, `redeemSkipping` and `claim` pick an actor that holds shares or a claim instead of mostly returning early. `fail_on_revert` is now `true`: 64 runs at depth 300 on three seeds make 19,200 calls each with no revert, and the four invariants hold with the new rule. |
| LR-07 | LOW (scripts, docs) | Propose mode refused a delegated owner key; the testnet recipe waited from the wrong step; stale sentences | FIXED | `ConfigureWhitelist.propose` rejected any owner with code, and a key delegated under EIP-7702 has 23 bytes of it and still signs; `isDelegatedAccount` moved to `ScriptUtils` and both scripts use it (`test_LR07_proposeAcceptsAnOwnerKeyDelegatedUnderEip7702`). The mint-currency check named mainnet's USDC and USDT only; it now also refuses the rule's own USDC (`test_LR07_theRulesOwnUsdcIsNotProposedAsAComponent`). Listings need liquidity through the whole 30-minute window, so they must be proposed 30 minutes after `seed`, not `create` (on a Sepolia fork, four of five proposals were rejected with `ThinPriceHistory`): README, the `SetupTestMarket` header and its log line. Stale sentences corrected: README on who may restart after an expiry and on zero-amount burn legs, ARCHITECTURE on an executor using donated surplus. |
| LR-08 | INFO (tests) | The fork whitelist check never validated entries whose pool has too few slots | FIXED | `PoolHistoryTooShort` is the first rejection, so for most default pools the entry's minimum liquidity and supply were never checked on-chain. `_checkBehindTheSlotCount` answers the slot count as the minimum and runs the rest of `checkConfig` on the pool's real window; only `OLD`, a thin pool that traded within 30 minutes, is tolerated. Passes at block 26,005,292. |
| LR-09 | LOW | A price near zero next to one above `MAX_EXACT_PRICE` still panicked the snapshot | FIXED | Found by stress-fuzzing the LR-02 rewrite over the full tick range; the old rule fails the same input, so it is a leftover of XA-23. The unclipped units of the low-priced component, value over price, did not fit in 256 bits, and `targetUnits` panicked instead of clipping at double: a `startRebalance` that reverts while a mean tick sits at an extreme. `_clip` compares a share with the bounds as a value and turns it into units only between them. Test: `test_LR09_aNearZeroPriceNextToAnExtremeOneDoesNotOverflow` (panics 0x11 on the old rule); 30,000 stress runs over ticks of ±880,000, units up to `uint96`, keep masks and both methodologies no longer panic. |

Reviewed and found sound, with nothing to change: issue, redeem and claim rounding against the backing invariant;
the `held` bound in `_checkBacking`; the auction multiplier and the `uint96` headroom; the restart hold-off; capped-weight

`Deploy.checkOwner` against a real Safe 1.4.1; the CI workflow including its coverage parser; the committed ABIs.
