# Review of 2026-10-31

A review of `spec/rebalance-state-machine` at `6a1b5db` read the settlement arithmetic, the backing invariant, the fee
arithmetic, the auction's state machine, the zap's and the builder's measuring, `MintRequests` and the keeper's
executor under adversarial sequences, and found no high or medium finding. It reported one low finding and two
informational ones, with proofs of concept in a scratch test file (`test/Audit.t.sol`). Its probes became the
regression tests below and the file was deleted. A third probe in it, a stranger rebalancing an index with no supply
at `MU_MAX`, was not reported, and is not a finding: with no shares there is no holder to lose, nothing is delivered,
and the start still waits out the index's interval.

Each id is `AU-…`. A fixed finding's test carries its id, as `AGENTS.md` gate 6 requires. Each test was run against
its fix reverted, on a byte copy restored afterwards, and failed: AU-1 on `FeeSharesMinted` naming the stranger, AU-2
on a preview that did not revert, AU-3 on the arithmetic underflow.

What the fixes cost. The hub's runtime grew from 24,370 to 24,478 bytes, 98 under EIP-170's limit: 21 for AU-1 and 87
for AU-2's wider calldata. An issue that names a referrer and pays a fee makes one more STATICCALL to the factory. Three
of `DeployWeighted`'s release hashes moved (CF-5): the factory's, the zap's and the builder's, whose sources import the
interfaces whose comments changed; `MintRequests`'s did not. The Sepolia deployment of 2026-10-26 (`07aeae1`) keeps the
old preview; the keeper bot runs on chain 1 only, so nothing reads it there.
