# Design decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Chain | One deployment, on Ethereum. Nothing crosses a chain inside a protocol contract: coins from other chains are held as the Ethereum tokens that represent them. The keeper's executor, which holds no role, calls Wormhole's Token Bridge to restock them. |
| Composition | 2 to 100 components per index, fixed at creation with their weights (basis points summing to 10,000). A new list or new weights is a new index. |
| Creation | Only a wallet the factory's owner approved creates an index (`setApprovedCreator`, at once; `CreatorNotApproved` otherwise). `createIndex(name, symbol, components, params)` takes one `ComponentSpec {token, weight, units}` per component. The app derives the starting units from the weights at its own prices; each lies between `MIN_RAW_UNITS` and `maxUnitsOf(token)`. Nothing reads a price, so no contract can compare those units with the weights, and an index created off its weights loses that distance to whoever fills its auctions: 15.5% of a 50/50 index created 90/10 by value in one fill, at the worst a fifth of an index per fill. The approval keeps a stranger from creating one; the team creates on the weights. It is read at creation and nowhere else: it reaches no existing index and gives no power over funds, and an index's creator role still moves to any account, which is how a partner takes over an index the team made. A listing records only the token's decimals, which bound the units an index may start with. |
| Rebalancing | Once the index's interval (7 to 91 days, chosen at creation) has passed, anyone starts a rebalance, a plain call. After 2 hours of notice, a 4-hour auction lets any contract settle it: the executor names every component's new units, and the index accepts them when each leg keeps at least four fifths and at most twice its units and a weighted gain in units clears the auction's bar, which starts at a half and halves every twelve minutes. An accepted fill never lowers the weighted product of the units, so at any prices the index is worth at least `e^(bar − D)` times what it was, `D` its distance from its weights. |
| Prices | None. No contract reads a price, a feed, a pool's price or an oracle: not a rebalance, an issue, a redeem or a claim. A fair rebalance price needs an executor bidding (ours first); a lone executor takes the index's distance from its weights out of the holders' value (0.3 to 0.6% in an ordinary week, 5.0% after one coin of two doubles), and the creator of an index made off its weights can take up to a fifth of it per fill, so the app must not offer such an index. The app values an index off chain, and the keeper prices its own trades off chain. |
| Swaps | Uniswap's Universal Router 2.0.0, V3 or V4 pools, one venue per leg, chosen by the caller. The zap fits about 35 components in one transaction (EIP-7825's 16.78M gas cap); the basket builder covers wider baskets over several. |
| Fees | Paid in shares, never in components. The creator's rate (0 to 1%) plus the platform's (0 to 0.25%, the policy when the index was created) on every issue and redeem; a referrer named at mint takes a share of the platform's part. An optional streaming fee (creator up to 5% a year, platform up to 1%) accrues in shares and moves no tokens. |
| Access | A gated index issues only to its creator and the recipients its creator allows, until the creator opens it for good; a referrer it would refuse gets no share, which stays the platform's. An unlisted index is simply not shown by the app. |
| Assets | Ethereum ERC-20s: the issuer's own contract where Ethereum has one, Bitcoin as WBTC, and otherwise the copy Wormhole's token bridge wraps from the coin's home chain. Tether Gold and Ondo's tokenized stocks are listed too. The labels the app must show (Native, Bridged, Custodial, issuer powers) are in [assets.md](assets.md). |
| Stablecoins and ether | Mint with USDC, USDT or ether through the zap, the basket builder (stablecoins), or a limit order in `MintRequests`. Burn to USDC, USDT, ether or any other token, or in kind. Index tokens are fungible, so the protocol does not track which stablecoin a holder used. |
| Governance | No admin can move an index's funds, mint or burn its shares, pause or upgrade it, or change its units or weights. The owner, a Safe that needs at least 3 signatures, proposes listings (effective after 2 days) and guardian changes (after 7), and sets the fee policy for new indexes. The guardian can cancel pending listings, delist, cancel a rebalance, and start one around a frozen component. Ownership moves in two steps and cannot be renounced. |
| Frozen components | If an issuer pauses or blacklists a component, holders take every other component out with `redeemSkipping` and claim the frozen one later. The guardian rebalances around it with a keep mask, which neither moves the component nor reads its price. |

## Not built yet

- `createAndMint` in one call.
- Constituent changes: a new list is a new index.
- Legs that split one swap across V3 and V4, and per-pair partial-fill auctions.
- A backstop for auctions too small to pay. The keeper fills only what pays, unless its operator sets a subsidy.
- An aggregator or Universal Router 2.1 zap, and V4 pools on the Sepolia test market.
- An L2 deployment, if cheap minting for small buyers matters after launch.
