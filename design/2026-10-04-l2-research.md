# Ethereum L2s: what they would add to an index, and whether Ethereum can see them in time (research, 4 October 2026)

The owner asked whether Xindex can support the chains on a "chains by value" ranking, then asked for Ethereum's L2s
to be researched. This record answers two questions. What would an index gain from L2s? Could the hub learn an L2
vault's state within the time the design allows? Facts were checked on 4 October 2026, by L2BEAT, the chains' docs,
contract source, DefiLlama's and GeckoTerminal's APIs, and the 29 September snapshot in `design/analysis/data`. A fact
the research could not confirm is marked unverified.

## Verdict

- **The ranking measures deposits, not tokens.** Its dollar figures are DefiLlama's DeFi TVL per chain, to the cent
  (Ethereum $53.64B, Base $6.38B, Arbitrum $1.42B, OP Mainnet $0.50B; `api.llama.fi/v2/chains`, checked here). That is
  mostly bridged ETH, stablecoins, wrapped BTC and staking tokens in lending and DEX protocols. The names beside each
  chain are CoinGecko's 24-hour top gainers, micro-caps.
- **Tokens issued on L2s are 0.62% of the holdable universe, and none is investable for the flagship.** 38 L2-home
  tokens come to $14.7B. RAIN (Arbitrum, $8.8B) is the only one above the 0.2% floor, and it fails the liquidity screen
  (0.07% daily volume, about $0.6M of DEX depth). Without it, they are $5.9B (0.25%); the largest, ARB, is 3.3 times
  below the floor. Base's 21 tokens, mostly AI-agent tokens and memecoins, are 0.14%.
- **Optimistic rollups cannot meet the hub's deadline.** An order must be proven by its deadline plus `GRACE`, 6 hours,
  from an L2 block after the deadline; a rebalance's deliveries likewise. Base needs 1 to 5 days to be final on L1, OP
  Mainnet, Ink and Unichain about 7, Arbitrum One and Robinhood Chain 6 days 8 hours. Mints on them would always fail.
- **ZK rollups can, but hold nothing an index wants.** Scroll is final on L1 in about 2 hours and its state is a
  Merkle-Patricia trie our library already reads. Linea (about 2 hours) and ZKsync (about 4) fit with a new tree
  verifier, Starknet (1.5 to 3 hours) with a Cairo vault. Their own tokens are SCR $5M, LINEA and ZK on L1 already. All
  are Stage 0, or Stage 1 under a council that upgrades at once.
- **The L1 route is nominal too.** The L2 gas and governance tokens that are already Ethereum ERC-20s (ARB, OKB, MNT,
  POL, STRK, IMX, WLD, LINEA, ZK) have $0.02M to $3.9M of L1 pool depth, against about $50M for UNI and LINK. They are
  listable, but the zap could not route meaningful size.
- **Recommendation: build nothing for L2s.** The value is about 0.2% of the universe outside RAIN, the fast-finality
  chains do not hold it, and the chains that hold it cannot be proven in time. Revisit if Base's ZK proofs become the
  rule for every game, or Arbitrum's ZK settlement ships, or an L2-home token reaches the floor with L1 liquidity.

## 2. Whether Ethereum can see an L2 vault in time

**The budget.** `HybridHub.failOrder` and `HybridHubModule.failRebalance` open at `deadline + GRACE`, 6 hours
(`HybridHubStorage.GRACE`). The state must come from an L2 block at or after the deadline, so that block must be final
on L1, and its proof built and sent, within 6 hours. `MAX_ORDER_LIFE` only adds time before the deadline.

| Chain | Type | Stage | How L1 learns its state | Final on L1 (typical / worst) | State format | Fits 6 h? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Base | own OP-derived stack (Azul), multiproof: AWS Nitro TEE and SP1 | 1 | `AggregateVerifier` games; OP output root | 1 day with both proofs, else 5 (docs say 7) / 5 after a challenge | MPT | no |
| OP Mainnet, Ink, Unichain | OP Stack, permissionless fault proofs | 1 | `SuperFaultDisputeGame`; super root, then output root | about 7 days / over 10 | MPT | no |
| X Layer | OP Stack, OP Succinct Lite | other | one whitelisted proposer and challenger | 1 hour + 3.5 days / more | MPT | no |
| Mantle | OP-derived, OP Succinct validity proofs | 0 | `OPSuccinctL2OutputOracle`, 12-hour challenger window | about 13 hours / frozen if the proposer stops | MPT | no |
| Morph | optimistic, ZK on challenge | 0 | `Rollup.finalizedStateRoots` | 2 days / +3 | MPT since April 2026 | no |
| Arbitrum One | Nitro, BoLD | 1 | confirmed assertion; `Outbox.roots(sendRoot)` is the L2 block hash | 6 days 8 hours / 12 days 17 hours | MPT | no |
| Robinhood Chain | Arbitrum Orbit L2 on Ethereum, 2 whitelisted validators | not ranked | as Arbitrum | as Arbitrum | MPT | no |
| Scroll | ZK rollup | 0 | `ScrollChain.finalizedStateRoots` | about 2 hours | MPT | **yes**, with `MerklePatricia` |
| Linea | ZK rollup | 0 | `LineaRollup.stateRootHashes` | about 2 hours | sparse Merkle, MiMC | yes, with a new verifier |
| ZKsync Era | ZK rollup | 0 | batch commitments | about 4 hours | Blake2s tree, EraVM | yes, with a new verifier or L2-to-L1 logs |
| Starknet | ZK rollup | 1 | core contract `updateState` | 1.5 to 3 hours / frozen if the operator stops | Patricia over Pedersen and Poseidon | yes, with a Cairo vault |
| Polygon PoS | sidechain, checkpoints by two thirds of stake | other | `RootChain` checkpoints of transaction and receipt roots, no state root | about 30 minutes | receipts only | on time, but committee trust like BNB's, through attested receipts as TRON's |

**Faster routes that exist, and why they do not change the verdict.**
- A ZK-proven game before its delay on Base, X Layer or Mantle needs every game from the anchor to the target proven,
  at least a day back on Base. At OP Succinct's 2024 price of about $0.62 a Base block, that is roughly $27,000 a day
  (no current figure). It also gives up the airgap and the blacklist, and trusts each chain's verifier keys and
  multisig, which on Mantle can switch proofs off at once.
- Proving OP Mainnet or Arbitrum ourselves means proving at least 7 days of history, or continuously, and reworking the
  prover at every hard fork.

**Upgrade keys.** Every chain above can be upgraded by a multisig or council without an exit window, except OP
Mainnet's regular upgrades (a 7-day veto since August 2025). A coin in a vault on an L2 rests on the same proof system
and keys as that L2's canonical bridge, so the vault adds no trust of its own, and removes none.

## 3. What building it would take

- **Token support in the vault.** An L2's coin is ETH, which the hub already holds as WETH, so a vault there is useful
  only for tokens. The factory and hub already name vault coins by a generic token id, and the vault's caps, holdings
  and deposits are keyed per token. Only deposit, refund and payout assume the native coin (`XindexVault.deposit`, the
  refund path, `_send`). By the twin rule, the change goes into all four vaults.
- **A state verifier per family:** Scroll's from `finalizedStateRoots` with the existing `MerklePatricia`; Linea's and
  ZKsync's with new tree verifiers (OpenZeppelin-audited provers exist in `openintentsframework/broadcaster`). Scroll
  and Morph commit a bare state root, so the vault would record `block.timestamp` in a slot.
- **P1 and P2 are not needed on a rollup.** The L2 already sees L1 block hashes with the rollup's own trust (OP Stack's
  `L1Block` predeploy; pushers on Arbitrum, Linea, Scroll and ZKsync), which the vault relies on anyway. Whether that
  holds across an L1 reorg is unchecked.
